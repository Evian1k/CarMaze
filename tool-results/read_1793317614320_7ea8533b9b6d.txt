  1488→## 16. Required acceptance tests
  1489→
  1490→Do not consider this module complete until these scenarios pass:
  1491→
  1492→**Scenario A — Stolen vehicle listing**
  1493→
  1494→A user attempts to list a vehicle with conflicting identity information. The system flags the conflict, blocks publication where necessary, creates a review case and records the evidence.
  1495→
  1496→**Scenario B — Impersonating an owner**
  1497→
  1498→A user attempts to sell a vehicle without sufficient ownership authority. The system requests additional verification and prevents an unapproved listing from becoming public.
  1499→
  1500→**Scenario C — Fraudulent test drive**
  1501→
  1502→A suspicious user attempts to book or collect a vehicle without satisfying the required checks. The showroom cannot release the vehicle through the normal workflow.
  1503→
  1504→**Scenario D — Garage vehicle handover**
  1505→
  1506→A customer submits their vehicle for repair. The garage receives it, records the condition and mileage, identifies the receiving employee and provides a digital receipt.
  1507→
  1508→**Scenario E — Unauthorised road test**
  1509→
  1510→A technician attempts to drive a customer's vehicle without the required permission. The system blocks the unauthorised workflow and records the attempt.
  1511→
  1512→**Scenario F — Vehicle not returned**
  1513→
  1514→A garage has custody of a customer's vehicle, but the customer reports it missing. The system opens a security incident, preserves evidence, alerts authorised reviewers and prevents the garage from silently closing the case.
  1515→
  1516→**Scenario G — Unauthorised collection**
  1517→
  1518→A person attempts to collect a customer's vehicle without valid authorisation. The garage cannot complete the release workflow.
  1519→
  1520→**Scenario H — Staff misconduct**
  1521→
  1522→A former garage employee attempts to access a customer's vehicle records. Access is denied and the attempt is logged.
  1523→
  1524→**Scenario I — False accusation**
  1525→
  1526→A business receives an unverified theft allegation. The system restricts sensitive information, enables a fair investigation and appeal, and does not publicly declare guilt.
  1527→
  1528→**Scenario J — Tenant isolation**
  1529→
  1530→One garage cannot access another garage's vehicles, customers, evidence, staff or incident records. One showroom cannot access another showroom's private records.
  1531→
  1532→**Scenario K — Evidence integrity**
  1533→
  1534→A user attempts to delete or alter a previous handover record. The original evidence and audit history remain preserved.
  1535→
  1536→**Scenario L — Customer notifications**
  1537→
  1538→The customer receives the correct notifications for handover, road-test approval, collection and security incidents. Failed notifications are recorded and retried appropriately.

  1542→## 17. Implementation instructions
  1543→
  1544→Before implementing:
  1545→
  1546→1. Inspect the current CARMAZE repository, architecture, database, authentication, existing garage and showroom portals, My Garage and notification systems.
  1547→2. Identify what already exists and reuse it.
  1548→3. Do not duplicate existing functionality.
  1549→4. Prepare safe database migrations.
  1550→5. Implement the security workflows in the real backend and frontend.
  1551→6. Integrate with existing roles, permissions and organisation-level tenant isolation.
  1552→7. Build responsive customer, garage, showroom and admin interfaces.
  1553→8. Add audit logs, notifications, evidence storage and automated tests.
  1554→9. Run migrations, type checks, lint, builds, integration tests and end-to-end tests.
  1555→10. Fix discovered issues rather than merely reporting them.
  1556→
  1557→Do not use mock vehicle verification, fake government checks, simulated tracking, fabricated evidence, fake SMS delivery or fake security analytics.
  1558→
  1559→Where an external integration requires credentials or an authorised provider, implement the integration boundary, document the exact missing requirement and keep the status explicitly unverified until the real check succeeds.
  1560→
  1561→**Final objective:** Every vehicle listed, viewed, test-driven, handed to a garage, worked on, moved and returned through CARMAZE must have an appropriate level of identity verification, authorisation, accountability and traceable history. Protect customers without making legitimate businesses impossible to operate, and protect businesses without exposing customers to unnecessary risk.
