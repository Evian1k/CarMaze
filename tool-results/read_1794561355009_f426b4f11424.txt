    41→## 3. Implemented in this session
    42→
    43→**A. Secure uploads (Phase 1)** — `POST /api/upload`: authenticated; 60/15min rate limit; JPG/PNG/WEBP allowlist (+PDF for `kind=document`); magic-byte sniffing with declared-vs-actual anti-spoofing; 8/10 MB caps; server-generated names `<userId>.<16hex>.<ext>` (exclusive-create write, collision retry); private documents stored separately and served only to owner/staff via `/api/files`; public media via `/api/media`; every upload recorded (`UploadRecord` model: owner, purpose, visibility, verified MIME, size) and audit-logged (`FILE_UPLOADED`). Malware-scanning hook point documented (`scanForMalware`) — no scanner configured in sandbox, stated honestly.
    44→
    45→**B. Verification-code lifecycle (Phase 2.2)** — codes now expire (10 min), carry a 5-attempt cap (then invalidated), are single-use, request-limited (5/h) and confirm-limited (10/h) for both email and phone; a daily job clears expired leftovers. Tested: countdown, `TOO_MANY_ATTEMPTS`, correct-code-dead-after-cap, `CODE_EXPIRED` on backdated expiry.
    46→
    47→**C. Session revocation (Phase 2.3)** — sessions carry a `sessionVersion` claim verified against the database on every request. Password reset and MFA-disable increment it: every previously issued session dies instantly (verified: old cookie → `{user:null}`); the acting user is re-issued a fresh session (demo sessions preserve their demo claim).
    48→
    49→**D. Central entitlements (Phases 5/11)** — `src/lib/entitlements.ts` resolves every plan limit from the seeded Plan rows (dealer/garage columns; supplier product/warehouse limits parsed from the seeded feature strings — "Up to 50 products"/"1 warehouse"/"Unlimited"), with deny-by-default free tiers and a 3-day grace window before `PAST_DUE`. Enforced server-side: supplier product creation (`PLAN_LIMIT_REACHED` on breach) and warehouse/branch creation; dealer listing + staff limits already enforced in their routes.
    50→
    51→**E. Scheduled operations (Phase 8)** — jobs runner now covers: application SLA sweep (hourly), subscription expiry → `PAST_DUE` with owner notice (hourly), campaign expiry → `ENDED` (hourly), reset-token cleanup (daily), verification-code cleanup (daily). All idempotent, audit-logged, `ENABLE_JOBS`-gated, hot-reload-safe.
    52→
    53→**F. Privacy readiness (Phase 10)** — `GET /api/privacy/export`: Kenya DPA data-subject export of the caller's own records (self-scoped queries, no hashes/other users, download audit-logged). `POST /api/privacy/delete-request`: records an erasure request as a DPO-tagged support ticket + staff notification — deliberately NOT auto-deleting (financial/fraud/dispute records have lawful retention); documented for legal review.
