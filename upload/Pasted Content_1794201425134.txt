# PHASE 4 — COMPLETE GARAGE VEHICLE CUSTODY PROTECTION

PRIORITY: CRITICAL

CARMAZE must protect customers when their vehicles are handed to garages.

**NO VEHICLE MOVES WITHOUT ACCOUNTABILITY.**

Preserve the existing custody lifecycle and strengthen its controls.

### Vehicle handover

- Customer and vehicle identity.
- Registration number.
- VIN/chassis information where available.
- Vehicle condition.
- Existing damage.
- Odometer reading.
- Fuel level.
- Declared valuables.
- Handover photographs.
- Work requested.
- Agreed spending limit.
- Expected completion date where available.
- Receiving staff member.
- Date and time.
- Customer acknowledgement.
- Garage acknowledgement.

Generate a handover reference and a customer-accessible record.

### Vehicle movement

Every authorised movement must record:

- Vehicle.
- Responsible person.
- Reason for movement.
- Permission granted.
- Date and time.
- Starting mileage where applicable.
- Ending mileage where applicable.
- Condition evidence.
- Outcome.

### Road tests

Implement strict authorisation.

A repair permission must not automatically grant permission for personal vehicle use.

A road test requires:

- Customer authorisation.
- Approved purpose.
- Assigned driver.
- Garage approval.
- Start time.
- Expected return time.
- Odometer readings.
- Return condition.
- Recorded completion.

### Additional repair work

Implement a real customer approval workflow.

When a technician discovers additional work:

1. Create an additional-work approval request.
2. Explain the work required.
3. Show the additional price or estimate.
4. Explain any expected delay.
5. Notify the customer.
6. Allow the customer to approve or decline.
7. Record the decision and timestamp.
8. Prevent unauthorised additional billable work.

### Vehicle collection

Strengthen the collection process.

- Verify the authorised collector.
- Require a secure, single-use collection reference.
- Record the final vehicle condition.
- Record final mileage.
- Record outstanding issues.
- Record the responsible releasing staff member.
- Record customer confirmation.
- Preserve the collection evidence.
- Prevent unauthorised collection.

Collection codes must have attempt limits, expiry, and secure storage.

A garage must not be able to erase or silently rewrite historical custody events.

### Disputes

Customers must be able to dispute:

- Missing vehicles.
- Unauthorised movement.
- Unauthorised road tests.
- Missing valuables.
- Unexpected damage.
- Unapproved work.
- Incorrect collection.
- Disputed vehicle condition.

Preserve evidence and audit history.

AI may prioritise suspicious activity, but it must never independently declare a customer, garage, or employee guilty.
